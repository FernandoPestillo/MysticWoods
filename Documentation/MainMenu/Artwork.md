# Arte da tela inicial

Gerada com a ferramenta integrada imagegen. Os arquivos finais ficam em `Assets/UI/MainMenu/Art/`.

## Fundo: enchanted-forest.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: finished 16:9 landscape background for the main menu of a pixel-art fantasy adventure called Mystic Woods. Create ONLY the background, no letters, no logos, no interface elements, no characters. Rich deliberate pixel art, old-school premium 2D RPG mood, visible crisp square pixel clusters and restrained dithering, not a smooth painting. A magical woodland at blue-green twilight: giant branching trees frame both edges, multiple receding layers of slender trunks and luminous mist, ferns and tiny foliage in the foreground, soft golden-green shafts of light and a few warm fireflies. Emerald green, deep teal, forest black and muted mint highlights with tiny gold accents. An inviting forest clearing or quiet path fades into the distance in the lower middle. The central 45% of the image must remain dark, low-detail and uncluttered so a large title and four readable buttons can overlay it; strongest interesting foliage and lighting around the outside edges. Beautiful atmospheric depth, calm and mysterious but welcoming. Full-bleed wide 16:9 composition, high-quality final game artwork. No text, no border, no HUD, no watermark.

## Letreiro: mystic-woods-title.png

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: transparent PNG title logo for a pixel-art fantasy forest adventure. Exact text, spelled correctly: "MYSTIC WOODS". Two centered lines: MYSTIC above WOODS. Design bespoke hand-drawn chunky pixel-lettering, expressive fantasy serifs and slightly irregular chiseled shapes, with very clean readable letter silhouettes. The letters themselves must be luminous YELLOW GOLD (#FFD84A), with lighter buttery yellow upper edges, restrained amber shading, a deep dark forest green-brown outline and a small dark drop shadow that makes the wordmark readable over a forest. Art-directed rich crisp pixel art with coherent square pixel clusters; NO smooth vector/cartoon curves, no modern sans-serif. Compact and balanced horizontal logo, MYSTIC line a little smaller than WOODS, with only tiny restrained leaf-tip flourishes integrated into a few letter terminals. Letterforms are the main subject. No external frame, no wooden sign, no foliage backdrop, no additional symbol, no character, no other wording, no subtitle, no watermark. Actual transparent background, preserve alpha, not a checkerboard image. Tightly framed around the whole logo with modest transparent padding and no clipped shadows. This is the complete finished standalone yellow title art to overlay in Unity.
